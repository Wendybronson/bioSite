About David

David's Story

David describes himself as hardworking, honest, and someone who enjoys helping others. Family has always been important to him, and much of what he works toward is creating a secure future for those he cares about. He also finds satisfaction in helping others reach their goals and seeing them succeed.

Throughout his career, David has worked in several different areas, including manufacturing, management, industrial engineering, and real estate. One of the most meaningful parts of his career was his time at IBM. While working in industrial engineering, he was challenged with helping turn an empty building into a successful production operation. The experience allowed him to use problem-solving, planning, and leadership skills in a way that produced visible results.

Real estate and property ownership have also played an important role in David's life. His friend and mentor, Bill Devore, provided guidance when David first became involved in real estate and investing. Over time, David worked toward his goals of owning property and becoming financially free from debt. Reaching those goals is something he is especially proud of.

Outside of work, David enjoys spending time outdoors. Going to the lake, boating, walking, and simply observing nature give him an opportunity to relax and step away from everyday responsibilities. His love of the outdoors began when he was a teenager and spent time at the lake and beach. He also enjoys traveling and experiencing new places, including trips to Mexico and Alaska.

At the center of David's story is his commitment to family. One of his biggest goals is to continue creating financial security for his family while also enjoying the experiences and relationships that make life meaningful.

Personal Highlight

David takes the most pride in seeing the people he cares about succeed. He values family, friendship, and the opportunity to help others, and he continues to work toward creating a secure future for his family.
